# SETTLE finalist refinement — Interlock / Finality

**Status:** finalist family for human review only. No finalist variant has been selected as the final Settle logo.

## 1. Human Round-2 decision
Blind review selected `03`; reveal identified it as Candidate I — Interlock / Finality. G, H, and J remain preserved but are rejected as equal finalists.

## 2. Why Candidate I survived
I avoids an immediate Latin-letter reading, has a compact asymmetric silhouette, and makes two geometries appear to reconcile into a stable final state. It works monochromatically beside `Settle` and has clear Converge / Resolve motion potential.

## 3. I-0 geometry
Reference: `../round-2/candidate-i.svg`. Native 24×24 viewBox; two filled polygonal forms; hard corners; diagonal vocabulary approximately 32°/48°; broad seam and lower-left negative-space channel. I-0 is not modified or duplicated as a source asset.

## 4. I-1 changes — optical balance
`candidate-i1.svg` keeps the two-path construction. The upper field changes from `(8.2,2)/(15.4,6.1)` to `(8.4,2.1)/(15.2,6.0)`, while the lower-right terminal shortens from x=21.5 to x=21.0 and the lower field tightens its inner cut from `(11.8,12.9)` to `(11.7,12.3)`. This shifts visual weight inward, opens the central void slightly, and reduces the long-tail bias without symmetry or rounding.

## 5. I-2 changes — interlock clarity
`candidate-i2.svg` preserves the I-0 lower field and introduces one deliberate shared seam: the upper field uses a 7.1-unit shoulder and a controlled 3.4-unit interface cut; the terminal is shortened to x=21.2. The repeated diagonal relationship is kept, but the meeting geometry is more intentional and less coincidental. It is not a literal chain link or puzzle join.

## 6. I-3 changes — small-size silhouette
`candidate-i3.svg` is an optical small-size variant. The terminal is shortened/repositioned (`2.7`-unit terminal versus I-0's `3.0/3.3`), the upper diagonal is made less directional, and the inner channel is reduced by approximately 0.3 units. This lowers chevron/checkmark pull while retaining the two-mass family at 24–48px. No radius was added: raster tests showed the hard corners carry more identity and a radius added no material benefit.

## 7. Master vs optical-small decision
Recommendation: use I-1 or I-2 as the eventual master candidate at ≥24px, pending human selection; reserve I-3 for 16px/favicons only if product testing confirms improved distinctiveness. Do not force I-3 onto large applications.

## 8. Corner-language decision
A/B test: precise square/hard engineered corners versus a small optical radius. Hard corners win. They preserve the engineered interlock, remain clean in black/white, and avoid softening the mark into a generic app icon.

## 11–12. Light/dark and size results
All marks are flat single-color fills and structurally invert cleanly on light/dark grounds. At 64/48px all retain distinct two-part mass; at 32/24px all remain identifiable and brand-like; at 20px I-1/I-2 are strongest; at 16px I-0 technically survives but drifts toward check/chevron, I-1 reduces the drift, I-2 retains the seam but is denser, and I-3 is the recommended optical test. Technical survival is not treated as final brand distinctiveness.

## 13–17. Context and motion findings
The horizontal lockup uses exact `Settle` as neutral live text in the exploration board only; no font dependency is introduced into the mark files and no wordmark customization is proposed. The symbol should sit below the wordmark's visual dominance with a calm gap and centered baseline. Browser-tab simulations at 16/32 support I-3 as a possible optical icon, but are not favicon integration. The neutral header behaves as a serious infrastructure identity without navigation or marketing decoration. Large/deck scale gives I-1/I-2 sufficient presence beside “Programmable USDC settlement infrastructure.”

The motion board is static only: separated → approaching → interlocking → final. Concept duration is 600–900ms, with no bounce, loop, spring, or crypto-like behavior. Every final frame is intended to equal its static master geometry; motion remains secondary to static quality.

## 18. Product-motif relationship
Later Deterministic Branch visuals may reuse only the diagonal angle family, precise cut logic, hard-corner language, interface gap, and mass/line proportion. The product visualization must not copy the logo or turn it into a payout diagram.
