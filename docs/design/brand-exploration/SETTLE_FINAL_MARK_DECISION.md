# Settle final brand mark decision

## 1. Decision status

**LOCKED — D4D0.6B1-R3 final product and brand direction decision.**

The Settle brand-mark construction is selected. This record supersedes earlier recommendations about which candidate should advance, but it does not alter or rewrite the historical Round-1, Round-2, or finalist exploration documents. Those files remain an accurate record of the review process at the time they were produced.

This lock selects source constructions and usage roles. It does not create final public assets or integrate the mark into the product.

## 2. Decision date and context

**Decision date:** 2026-08-08
**Decision context:** D4D0.6B1-R3, following Round-1 rejection, Round-2 blind review, Candidate I selection, finalist optical refinement, finalist blind review, native 64/48/32/24/20/16 review, browser/header/deck context review, labeled reveal, and motion storyboard review.

The decision is authoritative human design judgment informed by the complete review sequence. Advisory scores and earlier recommendations do not override it.

## 3. Brand territory

The locked brand territory is **Convergent Ledger**.

The brand mark represents **two independent conditions reconciling into one stable final state**. It should communicate controlled convergence, finality, and infrastructure-grade precision without becoming a literal ledger, transaction path, chain link, letterform, or generic interface icon.

## 4. Master mark decision

The master mark is **I-0 — original Interlock / Finality construction**.

- Source: [`round-2/candidate-i.svg`](./round-2/candidate-i.svg)
- Default use: **24px and above**
- Construction role: canonical static brand geometry

I-0 is the source of truth for normal-size brand use. It must not be replaced by I-3 at normal sizes.

## 5. Optical-small decision

The optical-small mark is **I-3**.

- Source: [`finalist/candidate-i3.svg`](./finalist/candidate-i3.svg)
- Use: **16–20px**, favicon, and very small UI contexts
- Construction role: size-specific optical adaptation, not a new master

I-3 exists to preserve the family silhouette and reduce directional or check/chevron drift where raster size materially constrains the original construction.

## 6. Why I-0 won

I-0 won because the original Interlock / Finality construction gives the clearest and most distinctive expression of the selected concept at normal sizes:

- two genuinely independent fields reconcile into one compact, stable mass;
- the broad offset seam communicates interlock and finality without literal rings or links;
- opposing directional biases settle into visual stillness rather than reading as ongoing routing;
- the asymmetric silhouette avoids an immediate Latin-letter reading;
- the original proportions preserve the strongest negative-space channel, tension, and large-scale character;
- it remains structurally clear in flat monochrome and works beside the neutral `Settle` name;
- it provides direct conceptual potential for Converge / Resolve motion while remaining complete as a static mark.

The finalist refinements improved particular optical attributes, but none produced a stronger all-size master than the original construction after blind, labeled, native-size, and context review.

## 7. Why I-3 is small-only

I-3 deliberately compresses and rebalances details to improve very-small-size behavior. Its shortened/repositioned terminal, less directional upper diagonal, and reduced inner channel help the mark remain compact and reduce checkmark or chevron pull at 16–20px.

Those same interventions remove some of I-0's characteristic tension, negative-space expression, and directional counterbalance when enlarged. I-3 is therefore an optical correction for constrained raster contexts, not a general improvement to the canonical geometry. Using it at normal sizes would flatten the qualities that made I-0 the master.

## 8. Rejected finalist refinements

- **I-1 — optical balance:** rejected as the master. Its inward weight shift and shortened terminal improve balance, but the human review retained the stronger character and original tension of I-0.
- **I-2 — interlock clarity:** rejected as the master. Its more deliberate shared seam increases interface explicitness, but I-0's broader, less explained relationship reads as the more distinctive brand construction.
- **I-3 — small-size silhouette:** rejected as the master but accepted as the optical-small mark. Its changes solve a size-specific problem and must remain limited to that role.
- **Rounded-corner alternative:** rejected. Radius treatment softened the engineered identity and produced no material optical benefit.

These outcomes do not require modification of the finalist exploration record; that document correctly preserves the pre-decision review state.

## 9. Size policy

| Rendered size/context | Required mark |
|---|---|
| 24px and above | I-0 master |
| 20px | I-3 optical-small |
| 16px | I-3 optical-small |
| Favicon and equivalently constrained UI | I-3 optical-small |

I-0 is the default whenever the intended rendered size is at least 24px. I-3 must not silently replace I-0 in headers, decks, lockups, marketing surfaces, or other normal/large contexts. Future asset production must label and route both constructions explicitly so consumers do not treat them as interchangeable.

## 10. Monochrome policy

The mark is fundamentally **flat and single-color**. It must work as black on a light ground and white on a dark ground with structurally equivalent geometry. The mark must not depend on gradients, opacity, filters, shadows, strokes, or multiple colors to communicate its construction.

Monochrome is the locked structural policy. A future color system may assign approved foreground/background colors, but color must remain optional and must not redefine, separate, or reorder the two fields.

## 11. Corner language

The locked corner language is **hard engineered corners**.

Corners must remain precise and faceted. Generic rounding is not part of the brand-mark construction; it weakens the interlock, softens infrastructure character, and moves the symbol toward generic app-icon language.

## 12. Wordmark status

No final wordmark has been selected or drawn. The neutral live-text `Settle` treatments used in exploration boards were context tests only; they do not establish a font, custom lettering, spacing specification, or converted wordmark asset.

Wordmark selection and lockup specification remain future brandkit work. No wordmark SVG is authorized by this decision.

## 13. Color status

No brand color palette or colored-logo treatment is selected in this record. Light/dark monochrome performance is validated, but exploration-board colors or backgrounds are not final brand colors.

Color definition, accessibility checks, reproduction rules, and any approved color pairings remain future work. No colored logo asset is authorized by this decision.

## 14. Motion identity

The primary motion identity is **Converge / Resolve**.

The intended behavior is:

- one-shot;
- controlled;
- approximately **650–800ms** as the target range for later implementation;
- no bounce;
- no spring;
- no loop;
- ending in complete stillness.

The conceptual sequence is separated conditions → controlled approach → reconciliation/interlock → stable final state. The last frame must match the relevant approved static geometry exactly. Motion is secondary to static recognition and must not imply perpetual processing, speculative energy, or crypto-style ornament.

Final motion design and implementation belong to **D4D0.8**. No animation is implemented by this decision.

## 15. Product-motif relationship

The product visual companion is **Deterministic Branch**.

It may share selected geometric DNA with the brand mark: diagonal-angle family, precise cut logic, hard-corner language, controlled interface gaps, and disciplined mass/line proportions. It must remain a product visualization with its own information architecture.

Deterministic Branch must **not** reuse the logo as a payout diagram, duplicate the mark as branching paths, or make the brand symbol carry transactional semantics. The relationship is familial, not literal.

## 16. Context-test results

- **Blind review:** Candidate I survived without relying on its name or rationale and was selected from Round 2; finalist review confirmed the original I-0 as the master direction.
- **Native sizes:** I-0 remains the stronger canonical construction at 24px and above. At 16–20px, I-3 gives the more controlled optical silhouette and reduces check/chevron drift.
- **Browser/favicon simulation:** I-3 is approved for favicon and equivalently small contexts; the simulation was evidence for the role, not favicon asset production.
- **Header context:** I-0 reads as a serious infrastructure identity beside `Settle` without requiring decorative framing.
- **Deck/large context:** I-0 retains distinctive negative space, tension, and presence better than the small-size adaptation.
- **Light/dark:** both selected constructions invert cleanly as flat monochrome forms.
- **Wordmark context:** the symbol works beside neutral `Settle` live text, but no tested type treatment is a final wordmark.
- **Motion storyboard:** Converge / Resolve supports the concept when it resolves once into exact static geometry and then becomes completely still.

## 17. What remains for D4D0.6B2

D4D0.6B2 is the brandkit/production-definition phase. It should:

- package authoritative master and optical-small vector assets from the locked sources without redesigning them;
- define asset naming, metadata, viewBox, export hygiene, and source-of-truth ownership;
- specify clear space, minimum size, placement, alignment, and misuse rules;
- define light/dark and approved monochrome reproduction assets;
- select and specify the wordmark/type system and horizontal/stacked lockups;
- define the brand color system and accessible foreground/background pairings;
- define when product surfaces select I-0 versus I-3;
- prepare an asset/export matrix for later favicon, PNG, ICO, app-icon, social/OG, and application integration work;
- document the boundary between the mark and Deterministic Branch product visuals.

D4D0.6B2 must preserve this construction decision. Public raster/icon assets and product integration are not created in this R3 record.

Motion implementation is separately deferred to D4D0.8.
